# 2026W37-S7-DRILL-B01 — H7 evidence note

Stream S7. Hypothesis H7: dynamic IFT, interfacial coverage and dispersion stability mediate HPHT filtration, lubricity and rheological stability better than MWCNT mass concentration alone.

Extraction status: `machine_extracted`. Full texts of paywalled papers were not read. Sci-Hub was not used.

## 1. Short answer

**FACT.** Multiple primary drilling-fluid experiments already show that MWCNT dose changes rheology, API/HPHT filtrate and friction, including non-monotonic filtrate versus concentration and cases where SiO2 or graphene outperform MWCNT.

**FACT.** Functionalization at a **matched mass fraction** (0.0095 wt.%) changes filtrate more than it changes friction (Alvi et al., 2018, DOI [10.1115/omae2018-78702](https://doi.org/10.1115/omae2018-78702), abstract).

**FACT.** In a CC BY invert-emulsion study, SWCNT at 0.1 wt.% reduced Turbiscan TSI more than the same paper’s qualitative MWCNT discussion, with BET/size reported for both lots (Lysakova et al., 2023, DOI [10.3390/en16196875](https://doi.org/10.3390/en16196875), HTML).

**INFERENCE.** H7 is **not** supported yet as a tested mediation claim: almost no drilling paper in this batch jointly reports (i) dynamic or equilibrium IFT with surface age, (ii) interfacial coverage, (iii) a method-specific dispersion metric, and (iv) HPHT filtration + lubricity + aged rheology on the **same** formulation matrix.

**HYPOTHESIS (H7 remains).** Interface- and processing-aware descriptors will beat mass fraction once those measurements are co-registered.

**UNKNOWN.** Dynamic IFT, contact-angle mode, cake permeability, and core-scale damage for MWCNT muds in the included set.

## 3. Inference and remaining hypothesis

**INFERENCE.** Closest prior art already occupies “MWCNT improves WBM/OBM rheology/lubricity/filtrate.” Novelty for Paper 2 cannot be that generic claim.

**INFERENCE.** Alvi 2018 is the strongest *public abstract-level* hint that **surface chemistry at fixed mass** changes filtration more than friction — a weak, non-causal analogue of H7, still without IFT/coverage mediators.

**INFERENCE.** Lysakova Energies 2023 plus the inaccessible 2023–2024 Lysakova ColSurA/JMS/JML cluster is the closest **processing + aspect-ratio + multi-endpoint** programme; it still does not, in the texts we could read, fit a mediation model with held-out formulations.

**HYPOTHESIS.** H7 remains the PI’s testable claim. A minimum distinguishing experiment is: matched mixing energy; pristine vs two functionalizations; measure TSI **or** sedimentation supernatant fraction **separately from** DLS; equilibrium **and** dynamic IFT with surface age; then HPHT filtrate, cake, lubricity, aged flow curve — and compare nested models `outcome ~ mass` vs `outcome ~ mass + interface + dispersion`.

**UNKNOWN.** Whether Lysakova 2024 J. Mol. Liq. ([10.1016/j.molliq.2024.125448](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.molliq.2024.125448)) already contains that mediation analysis. Abstract was **not** retrieved from Crossref, OpenAlex or Semantic Scholar.

## 6. Contradictions and negatives

1. **MWCNT vs other particles:** GNP better shale inhibition than MWCNT at 0.1 ppb (Aftab 2017). SiO2 1 wt% preferred over MW-CNT-nanomud for fluid loss (Abraham 2025).
2. **Base fluid can reverse lubricity-like benefit:** polymer mud simulated torque increased (Alssafar 2019).
3. **Non-monotonic filtrate:** low MWCNT ppb can **increase** OBM filter loss (Okoro 2019: 15 ml at 0.5 ppb vs 7 ml at 2.5 ppb).
4. **Hydrophobic MWCNT vs GO:** MWCNT gel up, fluid loss not improved; GO opposite (Mohideen 2019, [10.1063/1.5124631](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.5124631), abstract) — **maybe** include, qualitative.
5. **SWCNT more efficient than MWCNT** on TSI (Energies 2023) and in secondary citation of ColSurA 2023 — H8-relevant, not a drilling-only H7 mediation test.
6. **16.67% HPHT filtrate** appears in two OA abstracts (Fazelabdolabadi 2014; Sedaghatzadeh 2016) at nearly the same T, P, vol% — **do not count as independent**.
