# MWCNT drilling-fluid research program

## Position in InterFaceDB

MWCNT is a focused application and validation layer, not a second unrelated project.
The database should be used to test a causal chain:

`nanotube identity + liquid formulation + processing`

`-> dispersion state + interfacial adsorption/wettability`

`-> rheology + filtration + lubricity + formation damage`.

The commercially relevant result is a reproducible formulation window and decision
method, not the generic statement that carbon nanotubes improve a fluid.

## Lead novelty claim to falsify

Dynamic interfacial descriptors and processing-controlled dispersion explain drilling
performance better than MWCNT mass concentration alone. A non-monotonic optimum should
exist because additional interfacial activity eventually competes with aggregation,
retention and pore plugging.

Closest prior art already covers generic CNT effects on rheology, friction and
filtration. The study therefore needs matched processing controls, functionalization
characterization and a mediation link between interface state and engineering response.

## Minimal staged experiment

### Stage 0: feasibility and governance

- select one primary fluid class: water-based, hydrocarbon-based or invert emulsion;
- confirm MWCNT batches, publication rights, funding boundary and safe handling;
- measure blank fluid and a commercial-reference additive;
- calibrate delivered mixing/sonication energy rather than reporting device time alone.

Go only if batches are characterized, endpoints are measurable and confidential data
are separable from the publishable dataset.

### Stage 1: dispersion screen

Use a small factorial or D-optimal design over pristine and two or three functionalized
MWCNT states, several low concentrations, salinity/divalent ions and two or three
dispersant or polymer choices. Keep mixing order and specific energy controlled.

Minimum outcomes: time-resolved sedimentation or centrifugation yield, zeta potential,
microscopy or complementary size evidence, viscosity and dynamic/equilibrium tension.

Stop formulations that show irreversible aggregation, unacceptable viscosity or an
unsafe preparation route. Promote only formulations whose stability reproduces across
batches and aging.

### Stage 2: interface mechanism

For the survivors, measure contact angle on a controlled shale/mineral surface, droplet
size and interfacial structure or coverage. Test whether Hansen/COSMO descriptors,
dynamic tension and viscosity outperform total surface tension as predictors.

Go if an interface-aware model improves held-out prediction and the proposed optimum is
reproducible. A monotonic or null result is still publishable if processing and negative
results are rigorous.

### Stage 3: drilling validation

Evaluate finalists and matched controls using aged flow curves, oscillatory response if
available, HPHT filtration, cake morphology/permeability and lubricity. Core/shale
invasion or CT is a later validation, not an entry requirement.

The primary analysis is multiobjective: interfacial benefit versus stability,
hydraulics, filtration, friction, damage, dose, preparation energy, cost and safety.

## Metadata that cannot be optional

- CNT class, batch, purity, diameter, length/aspect ratio, BET and Raman defect metric;
- functionalization chemistry and quantified surface groups where possible;
- concentration with unambiguous basis and source-native unit;
- complete fluid composition, brine, pH and dissolved or gas phase;
- addition order, equipment geometry, power/delivered energy, time and temperature;
- aging duration and temperature;
- exact test method, standard, replicate count and uncertainty;
- negative endpoints: aggregation, pressure rise, retention, plugging and permeability
  loss;
- material, energy and handling cost plus waste route.

For drilling data, resolve whether `ppb` means pounds per barrel before any conversion.

## Minimum equipment and safety gate

Required for a defensible Paper 2: controlled mixing/sonication, tensiometry, contact
angle, colloidal stability characterization, HPHT rheology/aging, HPHT filtration and
lubricity. Raman/XPS/TGA/BET and microscopy can be shared facilities. Dry CNT handling,
spill/waste procedures and local occupational-safety review must be approved before
sample preparation.

## Commercial framing

Compare two delivery concepts rather than assuming a stable long-lived suspension:

1. a qualified concentrate with shelf-life and freeze/thaw specifications;
2. dry or predispersed functionalized MWCNT with closed on-site dilution.

The decision metric is cost per achieved operating envelope, including preparation
energy, storage, dosage, compatibility and disposal. Non-public technical claims remain
hypotheses until backed by cleared protocols and raw validation data.

## Paper 2 decision

Proceed as an experimental paper only when Stage 0 is closed. Otherwise keep the branch
as a database and computational design case, and do not promise HPHT or core results.
