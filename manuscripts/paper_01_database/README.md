# Paper 1 database and benchmark

Working claim: representing measurement method, phase context, uncertainty and provenance enables a more credible and transferable benchmark than a row-level surface-tension dataset.

## Planned evidence

- schema and controlled vocabularies;
- source-license and provenance model;
- extraction gold set and error analysis;
- public redistribution-safe database snapshot;
- classical and physical baselines;
- QSPR/tree and mixture-aware models;
- random versus component/system/family/temporal splits;
- uncertainty calibration and applicability domain;
- ablations for method and provenance metadata.

## Figure plan

1. Data and provenance architecture.
2. Coverage map by property, phases, chemistry, method and conditions.
3. Duplicate/group structure and split design.
4. Baseline and ML results across OOD tests.
5. Calibration and applicability-domain map.
6. Active-learning or gap-priority demonstration.

Candidate journals remain provisional: *Scientific Data*, *Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling*, *Journal of Chemical & Engineering Data* and *Fluid Phase Equilibria*. Recheck scope and quartile before submission.
