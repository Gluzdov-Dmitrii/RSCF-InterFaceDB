# Paper 2 MWCNT drilling-fluid application

Working claim: MWCNT drilling-fluid performance is governed by a measurable interfacial and dispersion state, not by nanotube mass concentration alone.

## Core causal model

`MWCNT identity + functionalization + liquid composition + processing -> dispersion/interfacial state -> bulk and drilling outcomes`

## Minimum experiment

- pristine, COOH and one controlled intermediate or alternative functionalization;
- matched no-CNT and reference-additive controls;
- fixed and recorded mixing energy and order;
- saline and elevated-temperature conditions relevant to the chosen formulation;
- dynamic/equilibrium IFT, contact angle, sedimentation or centrifugation, zeta-potential or complementary stability measure, droplet/aggregate imaging;
- flow curves, viscoelasticity, aging, HPHT filtration and lubricity;
- selected cake, invasion or permeability-damage characterization;
- replicated endpoints and a predefined statistical model.

## Novelty guardrail

Do not frame the paper as a generic improvement study. Test H5-H7 and compare with the close drilling-fluid and Pickering-interface literature. Keep the ionic-liquid route as a high-risk extension.

Candidate journals remain provisional: *Langmuir*, *Colloids and Surfaces A*, *Journal of Molecular Liquids*, *Geoenergy Science and Engineering* and *SPE Journal*. Recheck scope and quartile before submission.
